# Cooking recipes (game_cooking.swf): the rounds of each dish, in the movie's own MimicMiniGamePhaseData.txt
# layout (Cooking.CookingGameServer.OnLevelData). The server sends a recipe's rows to the movie as OnPhaseDataMsg,
# one row each. Lines starting with # are for people and are never sent.
#
# ID^GAME^SEQUENCE^DURATION^OBJECTIVE_ID^START_ID^END_ID^STAGE_TYPE^DATA1^...^DATA11
#   GAME      the recipe: the MiniGameData.txt id of its game (the Level of its Mimic.json entry)
#   SEQUENCE  the round, from 1; rows with the same GAME and SEQUENCE are alternatives, one picked each play
#   DURATION  seconds on the round's timer
#   DATA1     the round's ingredient (Cooking.CookingIngredient), or the dish for addIngredients, cook and stir
#
# STAGE_TYPE, and what DATA2 on mean (the movie clamps each to the range given):
#    1 tenderize       hits needed (1-30), start speed, acceleration (1-100), top speed (25-100), hits per smash (1-10),
#                      points per hit, hammer size + % (0-300)
#    2 chop            chops (1-20), clicks per chop (1-5), slices per chop (1-10), points per slice
#    3 smashFruit      smashes per fruit (1-15), fruits (1-6), hammer size + %, meter speeds (medium, high),
#                      hits per medium smash (2-10) and high smash (3-20), points per weak, medium and high smash
#    4 crack           cracks per egg, eggs (1-5), points per egg, hammer size + %, meter speeds (medium, high)
#    6 measure         good precision % (5-50), perfect precision %, points for good, points for perfect
#    7 stir            turns (1-50), % chance the arrow turns around, points per turn, bonus per turn the right way
#    8 saw             pieces, motion per piece (3-20), points per piece (500 and up)
#    9 grate           swipes (3-15), seconds the bowl takes to cross (1-15), how far it moves (0-480),
#                      points per catch, catches between cheers (3-10, 0 for 5)
#   10 slice           pieces, drift allowed for a bad, good and perfect cut, points per bad, good and perfect cut,
#                      pieces a perfect cut takes off (1-5)
#   11 cook            seconds before the heat starts (1-10), % cooked when light, medium, perfect and burnt,
#                      points for raw, light, medium, perfect and burnt pieces (DATA5 must not be 0)
#   12 cut             points per good cut, points per perfect cut
#   13 setHeat         (unused), points for the round
#   17 addIngredients  1 to list the ingredients in the order they were prepared (else last first), points per one
#
# The movie only has art for: slice Tumtumnip, Moonbean, Choychoy, Snowturnip, Nettleshroom, Frostradish,
# Glowtruffle, Onion; cut the eight fish; tenderize and saw Mystery, Bear, Bat and Spider Meat; grate Chugaroot,
# Chocolate, Honeytwig; chop the five leaves; smash the eight fruits and Tomato; crack Egg; churn (setHeat) Vanilla
# or Chocolate Ice Cream; measure the broths, Slugmud, Rootbeer and Spaghetti (its sauce); add to a Meat or Fish
# Saute, Sundae, Omelet, Meat Stew, Fish Soup or Spaghetti; cook the sautes and Spaghetti; stir the stew, the soup
# and Spaghetti. What was sliced, cut, grated, tenderized, smashed or chopped is what the add round asks for, so
# every recipe prepares something first (five things at most: the list shows five).
