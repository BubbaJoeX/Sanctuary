### Gameplay

What the server runs today, beyond logging in and walking around:

* **Combat** - Hostile NPCs fight back in the open world (`src/Resources/Mobs.json`), and the client's own combat mode shows them as enemies. Picking a fight switches a player to their combat job; job abilities, combat orbs, spheres and grenades hit, stun, root, sleep, knock back or scare mobs. A player a mob knocks out recovers at the zone's start after 8 seconds, unless an ally uses a Resurrection Scroll on them first: they get up where they lie with half their health, and mobs leave them be for 5 seconds.
* **Jobs and ranks** - Kills, quest rewards and finished collections earn stars for the active job, up to rank 20. Each rank needs 75 more stars than the last, adds 100 health and makes job abilities hit 5% harder, with the client's own level-up notice.
* **Loot** - Mobs roll a loot table (`src/Resources/Rewards.json`) for everyone who helped, at each mob type's drop chance. Loot bags such as the Thick Pocket Book open into coins, potions and scrolls.
* **Items** - 548 of the client's 686 usable items do something: potions, scrolls, buff foods, illusion foods, masks, fireworks, orbs and treats (`src/Resources/ItemAbilities.json`, generated by `ClientMods/extract_item_abilities.py`). Party crates, cakes, boomboxes and silly string come from `src/Resources/Consumables.json`.
* **Groups** - Invite to Group from a player's menu, or the client's group window, for groups of up to five: the first member leads, and group chat reaches every member. Members within 60 m of a kill share its stars, coins and loot roll.
* **Pathfinding** - Mobs, pets and NPCs walk the zone's navmesh (`src/Resources/NavMeshes`): close in, a mob runs straight at its target only when nothing is in the way, and knockbacks and flight stop at walls. The client's quest breadcrumbs get a navmesh route to the tracked objective. Zones with terrain but no navmesh get one built at startup (`ClientMods/zone_server_terrain.py` makes the terrain); Fabled Realms and Lavender Coast ship theirs.
* **Trading** - Trade from a player's menu: both sides offer items and coins, lock in, and accept, and the swap is saved in one go. Equipped and no-trade items stay put.
* **Hosted events** - Event staff (Mod and up) open an event at their spot with `!host open <event>`, which tells everyone online to type `!join`. Joining takes players there from anywhere, unless they're mid-fight or knocked out. `!host summon` gathers everyone who joined, `!host prize <event> <itemId> [qty]` and `!host coins <event> <amount>` reward them, `!host who` lists them, and `!host close` and `!host end` stop new joins and finish the event. `!event on/off <key>` still spawns or despawns the event's NPCs. Seasonal holidays (`src/Resources/Seasons.json`: Halloween from 20 September to 11 November, winter from 1 December to 6 January) are events that are on by themselves between their days, every year: their NPCs spawn as one starts and go as it ends, and `!event on` can still turn one on out of season.
* **Referee Picks and the admin site** - The welcome screen's Referee Picks panel holds up to four cards that teleport players to a point of interest or open the Coin Shop, house browser, friends list, Hero's Journal, My Stuff or achievements. Referees (Mod and up) edit them in game with `!pick`, or sign in with their game account to the admin site the WebAPI serves at `/admin` (`http://127.0.0.1:20040/admin` by default) and add, reorder, preview and save cards there. Both write the `RefereePicks` table, which the Gateway reads every 10 seconds, so players online get new picks without a restart. `!pick reset` and the site's Restore stock picks button bring back `src/Resources/RefereePicks.json`. Five failed sign-ins a minute from one address are refused; host the site behind HTTPS when it's reachable from outside.
* **Live world editing (admin API)** - The Gateway runs a small admin API for the OSFR AdminPanel (see [Admin API](#admin-api)). Referees sign in to the panel with their game account and change the running world: its 3D map editor (the zone editor, opened from the panel) shows each zone's players, NPCs and mobs as the client draws them, and props, world NPCs and placed models moved, turned, scaled, placed, copied or deleted there change in game at once for everyone in view and are saved. Any actor with a `Models.txt` row (every one the client can draw has one, from `ZoneEditor/add_model_rows.py`) can be placed as a still, solid model (the "Placed model" NPC, 45000 in `src/Resources/Npcs.json`) and later baked into the zone's own files. NPCs spawn there from their `Npcs.json` definitions (or from any actor, on a definition the editor adds), and referees set a placed NPC's name, look, animation, disposition, behaviour and speed, give it a conversation, flag it for a world event or season, and give anything placed conditions (hours of the game day, a quest's state, an item, referees only, an event or season) that decide which players see it. The editor's World tool adds warpstones (a warp destination on the atlas with its stone), map places, named areas, portals, barriers and seasons, and names them with Sanctuary's own locale strings (`src/Resources/LocaleStrings.json`, sent to clients in the locale client patch); its Env tool adds environments (a sky, minimap and atlas page for tiles). The panel also lists and moves players, messages and kicks them, broadcasts, locks the world, sets the message of the day and the clock speed, turns events on and off, reloads resources and runs chat commands as the referee's character. Every change goes to the `Audit` log, and each referee can undo their own edits.
* **Mysterious Merchant** - Cragara travels the open world (`src/Scripts/Zone/MysteryMerchant.lua`). Each hour she opens shop in the next of ten towns for 45 minutes, warns 5 minutes before she packs up, and then vanishes until the next hour. Chat announces each stop, and players who enter the world are told where she is. She sells the server's custom items for coins: the Retro Hover-Car, the OSFR Foam Costume, streetwear, sport underwear and two new hairstyle cards. NPC merchants sell everything on their own lists, not only what the coin store stocks, as long as the player is still in reach.
* **Minigames** - The Games window lists 194 Flash minigames the server plays along with (`src/Resources/Minigames`): mining, forging, mail sorting, harvesting, artifact hunts and Goo'd Candy on one match-three engine the server runs itself (it holds the board, so a level can't be faked); cooking, smelting, Feeling the Beat! and Hack 'n' Slash, which get their recipes and rounds from the server; tower defense; chess and checkers against the server's own engine; and the daily wheel. A win pays coins, stars to the game's job (Miner, Blacksmith, Chef or Postman) and what was made: the ore, bar, dish or forged item. A loss pays a share of the coins and stars. Referees open any game with `!minigame <id>` (`!minigame list` finds them), and an NPC or prop with the `minigame-table` script template opens one when clicked, for game tables and crafting stations. The client only loads a minigame's movie from its local packs, so `ClientMods/build_client_mods.py` and `install_client_mods.py` install the stock movies (see `ClientMods/README.md`). Every message to and from a movie is logged to `Logs/Minigames-<date>.log`.
* **Scripting** - Zone and GM-authored Lua scripts can run events with all of the above. They can read a player's health, job, rank, coins and group, take items and coins, give stars, warp players between zones, revive knocked-out players, hurt mobs or have NPCs attack players, open minigames, and react to mob hits, kills and respawns, players entering or leaving a zone, events starting and ending, and players joining a hosted event. See `src/Scripts/README.md`.
* **Also** - Quests and the Hero's Journal, collections, housing, pets, mounts, guilds, friends, the coin store and the Referee (GM) tools.

## Getting Started

This repository only contains the **server emulator** for Free Realms. To play the game, you must also have a **Free Realms client**. You can download the client using the **OSFR Launcher** available here: [OSFR Launcher](https://github.com/Open-Source-Free-Realms/Launcher).

### Prerequisites

Before you can use this software, ensure you have the following installed:

- **Visual Studio 2022**  
  Make sure to include the **.NET Framework development workload** during installation.

### Release

1. Clone the repo
   ```sh
   git clone https://github.com/Open-Source-Free-Realms/Sanctuary.git
   ```
2. Build the solution for `Sanctuary.Core` for `Release`
3. Create a file named `database.json` in the `Release` folder located within the new `bin` folder
4. Paste the following
   ```json
    {
    "Database": {
        "Provider": "Sqlite",
        "ConnectionString": "Data Source=D:\\Games\\Free Realms\\sanctuary.db;"
    }
   ```
5. Launch `Sanctuary.Login`, `Sanctuary.Gateway`
6. Connect to the client

**_IMPORTANT:_** Update the Data Source file path (D:\\Games\\Free Realms\\sanctuary.db) to match the location where your database files are stored.

**_NOTE:_** The following user should already exist, but if not then implement one with the following credentials:

```sh
1	admin	admin	EXmdPd5dbAcs58vZ0iCcPRtJkGdMePL2	10	0	1	1	2024-06-22 13:51:13.2736902+01:00	2024-07-14 01:57:45.8765645+00:00
```

### Debug

1. Clone the repo
   ```sh
   git clone https://github.com/Open-Source-Free-Realms/Sanctuary.git
   ```
2. Build the solution for `Sanctuary.Core` for `Debug`
3. Right-Click **'Manage User Secrets'** for the following projects:
   - `Sanctuary.Gateway`
   - `Sanctuary.Login`
   - `Sanctuary.Database`

4. Copy and paste the following configuration for **SQLite** into the secrets editor:

   ```json
   {
     "Database": {
       "Provider": "Sqlite",
       "ConnectionString": "Data Source=D:\\Games\\Free Realms\\sanctuary.db;"
     }
   }
5. Launch `Sanctuary.Login`, `Sanctuary.Gateway`
6. Connect to the client

**_IMPORTANT:_** Update the Data Source file path (D:\\Games\\Free Realms\\sanctuary.db) to match the location where your database files are stored.

## Usage

To spawn an npc ```/npc spawn <NameId> <ModelId> [TextureAlias]``` TextureAlias is optional

_For more examples, please refer to the [Documentation](https://github.com/Open-Source-Free-Realms/Sanctuary/wiki)_

### Admin API

The Gateway's admin API is off until it has a key. Give it one (at least 24 characters) in `src/Sanctuary.Gateway/gateway.local.json`, and the same key to the AdminPanel's backend (`Sanctuary:AdminApiKey`):

```json
{
  "AdminApi": {
    "Urls": "http://127.0.0.1:20050",
    "Key": "<a long random string>"
  }
}
```

It listens on loopback by default; the AdminPanel's backend is its only client, signing referees in with their game accounts and passing their requests on. Every request carries the key as a bearer token and, but for sign-in, names the referee acting (`X-Sanctuary-User`), whose admin or mod flag is checked on each request.

`launch.ps1` brings the panel up with the servers: a full launch rebuilds its backend (and its frontend's static export, when its sources changed) and every launch starts it at http://localhost:5057, with the zone editor its Map editor shows on :7050, unless they're running already. It looks for the AdminPanel checkout at `D:\FreeRealms\AdminPanel` (`-AdminPanelDir`); `-NoAdmin` leaves both out, and `-Stop` stops the panel too (the zone editor keeps running). Stopping only touches this checkout's servers (by their path under `src`) and the clients launched against them, so other checkouts' and worktrees' test servers keep running. Logs go to `run/adminpanel.log` and `run/zoneeditor.log`.

| Route | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /api/login` | Checks a referee's game username and password |
| `GET /api/status`, `PUT /api/settings` | Players online, world lock, message of the day, clock speed |
| `POST /api/broadcast`, `PUT /api/events/{key}` | Broadcasts (optionally as a banner, or to one zone); turns events on and off |
| `GET /api/players`, `POST /api/players/{id}/teleport\|message\|kick` | Players online and what a referee can do about them |
| `GET /api/commands`, `POST /api/command` | Chat commands, run as the referee's character in the world, with what they answered |
| `GET /api/zones`, `GET /api/zones/{key}/heightmap` | The running zones, and a zone's ground as a height grid |
| `GET /api/zones/{key}/live` | Server-sent events: players, placed objects and (with `?npcs=true`) the zone's other NPCs, with each one's look (its actors, texture and tint aliases and tints) |
| `POST /api/zones/{key}/props\|npcs\|models` | Places a furniture prop, a world NPC or any model |
| `PATCH\|DELETE /api/zones/{key}/objects/{id}`, `POST …/copy` | Moves, turns (yaw, pitch, roll), scales, renames, copies or deletes a placed object |
| `POST /api/zones/{key}/undo` | Undoes the referee's last edit in the zone |
| `GET\|PUT\|DELETE /api/zones/{key}/objects/{id}/script`, `POST …/script/use\|test` | A placed NPC's or prop's GM script (admins): read, save (it compiles and runs first, so a broken one never replaces a working one), remove, put a library script on it with its parameters, or test it on the referee's character, as the GM panel's Script tab does |
| `GET /api/scripts/library`, `GET\|PUT\|DELETE /api/scripts/library/{name}` | The script library (admins): the shipped templates and referees' saved scripts |
| `GET\|PUT /api/zones/{key}/objects/{id}/settings` | A placed NPC's, model's or prop's name, nameplate, animation, aliases, effect, height offset and icons, an NPC's disposition, mob, behaviour, speed and event, and anyone's conditions (hours, quest state, item, referees only, event), as one undo step |
| `GET\|PUT\|DELETE /api/zones/{key}/objects/{id}/conversation` | A world NPC's conversation (admins), as `!conv` builds it |
| `GET /api/npc-options` | What settings can be: the GM panel's animations, mobs, world events, seasons (with their days and whether they're on), quest states, the icons the game's NPCs use, and the game clock's hour |
| `POST /api/zones/{key}/definitions/{def}/respawn` | Respawns the zone's placed NPCs built on a definition, after `Npcs.json` was reloaded |

## Roadmap

- [x] Open-world combat, job ranks and mob loot
- [x] Potions, scrolls, buff foods, orbs and other usable items, the Resurrection Scroll included
- [x] Groups and player-to-player trading
- [ ] Crafting (recipes): the client's RecipeStart has no server yet, and the recipe data is missing
- [ ] Job minigames (Chef, Miner, Blacksmith...): the server only runs the daily wheel
- [ ] Fishing, matchmaking queues and housing ratings
- [ ] Combat pet summons (Bravery Biscuit, Creepy Construct Caller)

See the [open issues](https://github.com/Open-Source-Free-Realms/Sanctuary/issues) for a full list of proposed features (and known issues).
